Donkey Kong 3 - Atari 800XL adaptation / Atari 5200 port

This is an unofficial homebrew adaptation. Donkey Kong, Stanley, the original
game, and its original title design are Nintendo characters and concepts.
The executable does not contain or emulate the arcade ROM.

Music:
"Monkeys Spinning Monkeys"
Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original work:
https://incompetech.com/wordpress/2014/02/monkeys-spinning-monkeys/
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1400011

Composer-provided orchestral score:
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/sheetmusic/MSM%20Sheet%20Music.zip

Changes: reduced three-voice Atari POKEY arrangement of the opening 16 bars;
the principal flute melody is played one octave lower, strings become short
quiet accompaniment, and the excerpt loops. No original audio recording or
samples are embedded. The arrangement matches soundtrack audition 6, with
preview padding removed. In v0.12 the lead melody is reduced one POKEY volume
step, with audible level-one tails retained. PAL pitch divisors are adjusted
for the PAL clock.

The title screen contains the title, composer, source domain, license name
and URL, and notice that this is an Atari arrangement. This file provides
the full source links and adaptation details.
